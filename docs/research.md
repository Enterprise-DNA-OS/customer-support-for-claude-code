# Target evidence

Checked 15 October 2026. This is a customer support ticket operations base, distinct from the registry's IT service management and MSP operations builds. The helpdesk family had no completed build. Its earlier Freshdesk candidate was killed without a repo under the earlier generic-tool filter. The current charter admits expensive big-name tools.

## Candidate scores

Scores are ordinal judgments, not market measurements. Order: annual bill, data-shaped, our buyers, search demand, one-session buildable, import path.

| Candidate | Scores | Total | Decision |
|---|---|---|---|
| Zendesk | 4, 5, 2, 4, 5, 5 | 25/30 | Selected. Public seat price, documented CSV and a bounded offline operations workflow. |
| Intercom | 4, 4, 2, 4, 3, 4 | 21/30 | More dependent on live messaging and usage-priced delivery. Same broad family, not a next-build proposal. |
| Document360 | 1, 5, 2, 2, 5, 3 | 18/30 | Not eligible on the evidence available: the current pricing page is quote-based and no verified five-figure invoice was found. |

The Zendesk annual example is US$69,000 for 50 Suite Professional agents at the published US$115 per agent per month, paid yearly. It is arithmetic, not a customer invoice, a savings promise or an Enterprise DNA price. No confirmed Zendesk buyer or annual invoice was found in the local customer and CRM records. Search results showed multiple alternative list articles; search volume was not measured. Buyer fit remains a hypothesis to validate.

## Sources

- [Zendesk AU pricing](https://www.zendesk.com/au/pricing/): Suite Professional US$115 and Suite Team US$55 per agent per month, paid yearly. Current product tier descriptions and optional consumption costs.
- [Zendesk account exports](https://support.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/4408886165402-Exporting-ticket-user-or-organization-data-from-your-account): documented CSV columns, account timezone, enablement and export limitations. The fixture uses these headings with fictional records.
- [Intercom pricing](https://www.intercom.com/pricing): seat and usage pricing. [Seat documentation](https://www.intercom.com/help/en/articles/8205716-seats) states Expert US$139 per full seat monthly; 50 seats annually would total US$83,400 at that monthly rate, an illustrative calculation only.
- [Document360 pricing](https://document360.com/pricing/): tailored quotes rather than a usable public annual price.
- [Alternative search evidence](https://www.helpdesk.com/alternative/zendesk/): example of a competing list article, used only as qualitative evidence that alternative searches are contested. Its product claims were not adopted.
- [NZ privacy principle 5](https://www.privacy.org.nz/privacy-principles/5/) and [principle 9](https://www.privacy.org.nz/privacy-principles/9/): security and retention record checks, explained in compliance.md.

The page demonstrates questions against this build. It does not claim Zendesk is incapable of reproducing them or that this base reproduces every channel, service calendar or permission feature.
